# 🏚️ The Abandoned Cabin

A text-based survival adventure game built with Python.  

You wake up inside a dark, abandoned cabin. Your mission is simple: explore the rooms, collect money, find useful items, and survive long enough to escape.  

But be careful... there might be a crazy squirrel waiting for you! 🐿️  

## 🎮 How to Play

Your goal is to collect **$15** to escape the cabin.

You start with:

❤️ 10 Health
💰 $0 Money
🎒 An empty inventory

Each turn, you can choose one of three actions:

Action	Description
explore	Explore the cabin and discover random events.
rest	Recover health or drink a healing brew.
shop	Visit the shop and buy random items.
quit	Give up and leave the game.
⚔️ Game Features
Random Events: Encounter squirrels, discover money, find items, or explore empty rooms.
Health System: Keep your health above zero to survive.
Inventory System: Collect useful items like axes and healing brews.
Shop System: Buy random items at random prices.
Survival Gameplay: Make decisions to manage your health and money.
Win Condition: Collect $15 to escape!
### 🎒 Items
Item	Effect
🪓 Axe	Reduces damage taken from squirrel attacks by 1.
🧪 Brew	Restores 5 health when consumed.
🏆 Winning & Losing

You Win: Collect at least $15.

Game Over: Your health reaches zero or below.

Can you survive the abandoned cabin and find enough money to escape?

## 💻 Requirements
Python 3.x
No external libraries required.

The game uses Python's built-in random module.
